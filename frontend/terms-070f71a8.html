What Gridiron is

Gridiron publishes statistical predictions for NFL games, built from public play-by-play data. It reports win probabilities, simulated outcome distributions, play-calling tendencies, and fourth-down expected points. It is an analysis tool and a demonstration of method.

This is not betting advice
