Privacy policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

The short version. Slant has no accounts and no login. We do not sell data, run advertising, or build profiles. Analytics is off unless you switch it on, and even then it is anonymous and first-party. We do not collect any personal data by default.

Who we are

Slant is an independent NFL prediction project. It publishes model output built on open play-by-play data. It is not affiliated with the National Football League, any club, or any betting operator.

What we collect

  • Nothing, by default. You can read every page, open every game, and use every calculator without giving us anything. No account is required and none exists.
  • Anonymous usage counts, only if you choose "Allow analytics" in the cookie banner. This records which pages are viewed, not who viewed them.
  • Standard server logs, which include IP address and browser type. These are kept briefly for security and abuse prevention, then discarded.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, precise location, contacts, or any special category data. We do not fingerprint your device, track you across other sites, or use advertising cookies. There are no third-party trackers embedded in this site.

Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies to function. One value is written to your browser's local storage: your answer to the cookie banner, so we do not ask again on every visit. You can clear it at any time through your browser's site settings.

If you allow analytics, page-view counts are recorded on our own server. No third party receives them.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used here. Your browser requests them from Google, which will see your IP address as part of that request.
  • ESPN hosts the team logos and live scores shown on the board, which your browser loads directly from their servers.
  • nflverse supplies the underlying play-by-play data. We download it server-side; your browser never contacts them.

Legal bases

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies: we rely on consent for analytics, and on legitimate interests for security logging and abuse prevention. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting anything that happened before.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of any personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to processing, or request it in a portable form. In practice we do not hold any personal data about you by default.

To make a request, or to complain, contact us at the address below. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

How long we keep things

Server logs are kept briefly for security purposes. Analytics counts are aggregated and hold nothing that identifies a person.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note the change on the site. Continuing to use Slant after that means the updated policy applies.
