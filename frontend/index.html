Show the work

Most prediction sites give you a number and ask you to trust it. Here's what's actually inside ours: every feature's real weight, whether a "70%" call is really right 70% of the time, and how it's done against a real closing line every season since 2022 — not just the seasons that made it look good.

What actually moves the pick

Every game's driver breakdown is just this table applied to that matchup. Bar length is each feature's real, measured impact on the model's output — not a guess at what feels important.

Is a "70%" call really right 70% of the time?

This is calibration — the thing most confidence scores never get checked against. Games grouped by predicted probability; if the model is honest, the actual win rate in each group should land close to the predicted one.

Every season, backtested — including the bad ones

Walk-forward: each season is scored using only what the model could have known before it happened. Vegas is always shown next to it, because "we beat a coin flip" isn't the bar that matters.
